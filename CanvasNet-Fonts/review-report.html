1.2 Scope

This review covers the CanvasNet Fonts subsystem at the subsystem level only. The Fonts subsystem comprises two units, TrueTypeFont and SystemFontCatalog, each of which has its own separate unit-level review-set (CanvasNet-Fonts-TrueTypeFont, CanvasNet-Fonts-SystemFontCatalog). Per the hierarchical-scope principle in software-items.md, unit-level implementation detail is excluded from this review; this review instead relies on the subsystem design document’s description of what the two units provide, and verifies that the subsystem’s own requirements, design, verification-design, bundled third-party asset documentation, and shared test infrastructure are internally correct, mutually consistent, and consistent with the surrounding system-level context.
