### CoseLayout Unit Verification Design

This document describes the unit-level verification strategy for the `CoseLayout` unit in the
`DemaConsulting.CanvasNet.Mermaid.Cose` namespace: the internal sealed `CoseLayout` (the port of layout-base,
cose-base, and cytoscape-cose-bilkent as Mermaid runs them for a tree, cytoscape's straight-edge points, and the
correctly rounded sine, cosine, logarithm, and power, in six partial files) with the records `CoseSize`, `CoseLink`,
`CosePoint`, `CoseEdgePoints`, and `CoseResult`.

#### Verification Approach

The unit is verified against the browser in which Mermaid runs and rule by rule. The layout vectors
(`Fixtures/upstream-vectors/cose-layouts.json`, 36 layouts generated by `tools/visual-diff/mermaid/gen-cose-goldens.mjs`
in headless Chrome with the cytoscape, layout-base, cose-base, and cytoscape-cose-bilkent libraries extracted from the
pinned Mermaid bundle, running Mermaid's `createCytoscapeInstance` and layout options verbatim) give, for each tree, the
node centers, the edge points, and the iteration count, or the JavaScript error of the 3 cases Chrome cannot lay out; a
theory compares every value bit for bit. The math vectors (`Fixtures/upstream-vectors/js-math.json`, from the same
page) give Chrome's `Math.sin` and `Math.cos` of 23,480 angles (seeded angles in [0, 2π), angles in [-8, 8], and every
angle the layout cases used), `Math.log` of 5,002 values, and `Math.pow` of the 850 cooling pairs that layouts of 1 to
1,016 nodes reach, as hexadecimal bit patterns; the tests compare every result bit for bit. The vectors are generated
only in Chrome, never in Node, whose math differs. The unit tests then verify each rule on small inputs, with the
expected values stated in the tests as literals.

No mocking or stubbing is used; the meter is the real `LayoutWorkMeter`, created with the limit, location, and token
each test needs. The internal types are reached through the test assembly's `InternalsVisibleTo` access.

Unit tests reside in `CoseLayoutTests.cs` and `CoseGoldenTests.cs` within the `DemaConsulting.CanvasNet.Mermaid.Tests`
project, in the `Cose` folder.

#### Test Environment

- **Framework**: xUnit v3 running under the .NET SDK
- **Execution**: `dotnet test` invoked by `build.ps1` and the CI pipeline
- **Mocking**: None required
- **Fixtures**: `Fixtures/upstream-vectors/cose-layouts.json` and `js-math.json`, copied to the output folder
- **Isolation**: Each test lays out its own input; the vectors are only read

#### Acceptance Criteria

A unit-level test run passes when every scenario below passes without error or exception beyond those explicitly
asserted. Any center, edge point, iteration count, or math result that differs from Chrome's in any bit, and any
failure, work count, or special value that differs from the asserted one, constitutes a failure.

#### Unit-Level Test Scenarios

##### CanvasNetMermaid-Cose-CoseLayout-Tree: Only One Tree Is Laid Out

**Tests**: `CoseLayoutTests.CoseLayout_Run_NotOneTree_ThrowsArgumentException`,
`CoseLayoutTests.CoseLayout_Run_NoNodes_ReturnsEmpty`

Asserts that three nodes with one edge, with an edge to a node that does not exist, with two edges between the same
nodes, or with a self loop and a disconnected node are refused with an `ArgumentException`; and that no nodes and no
edges give an empty layout with 0 iterations.

##### CanvasNetMermaid-Cose-CoseLayout-StartPlacement: The Radial Start

**Tests**: `CoseLayoutTests.CoseLayout_Run_SingleNode_CornerAtMargin`,
`CoseLayoutTests.CoseLayout_Run_TwoNodes_HorizontalFromMargin`,
`CoseLayoutTests.CoseLayout_FindCenterOfTree_Lists_KeepsOddPositions`

Asserts that a lone node of 60 by 30 feels no force and ends at the center (45, 30), its corner at the margin of 15,
after the first convergence check at 100 iterations; that of two nodes of 80 by 40 and 120 by 40 the child is placed at
180 degrees from the center node, both centers at the height 35 and the child's at 75, the center node more than 100 to
its right; and that the center search gives the element 0 of `[0]` and `[0, 1]`, 1 of `[0, 1, 2]` and of five
elements, 3 of eight, and 7 of twenty (each pass keeps the elements at odd positions, as the original's splice loop
does).

##### CanvasNetMermaid-Cose-CoseLayout-Forces: The Forces And The Cooling

**Tests**: `CoseLayoutTests.CoseLayout_CoolingStart_NodeCounts_FollowsAdaptation`,
`CoseGoldenTests.CoseLayout_Run_GoldenTree_MatchesChrome`

Asserts that the cooling starts at 1 with an iteration limit of 2,500 for 1 and 500 nodes, 2,505 for 501 nodes, and
5,000 for 1,000 nodes, at `1 - 1/4000 * 0.67` with 5,005 for 1,001 nodes, and at 0.33 with 30,000 for 6,000 nodes; and
that the springs, the repulsion through the grid, the overlap separation, the moves, and the cooling give Chrome's
layouts bit for bit (the golden scenario below).

##### CanvasNetMermaid-Cose-CoseLayout-JavaScriptNumbers: JavaScript's Number Rules

**Tests**: `CoseLayoutTests.CoseLayout_ParseInt_WholeNumbers_FollowsJavaScript`,
`CoseLayoutTests.CoseLayout_Run_BeyondWorldBoundary_ClampsCorner`

Asserts that `parseInt` keeps 12, -7, and 999999999999999900000, gives 1 for 1e21, 2 for 2.5e22, and -3 for -3e25,
gives a positive zero for -0, and NaN for NaN and infinity; and that a lone node 3,000,000 wide ends at the center
(1998800, 35), because its corner is clamped to the world boundary when the drawing is moved (without the clamp it would
be at 15 + 1,500,000).

##### CanvasNetMermaid-Cose-CoseLayout-EdgePoints: Cytoscape's Straight-Edge Points

**Tests**: `CoseLayoutTests.CoseLayout_StraightEdgePoints_ApartCenters_EndsOnCircles`,
`CoseLayoutTests.CoseLayout_StraightEdgePoints_CloseCenters_NaN`,
`CoseGoldenTests.CoseLayout_Run_GoldenTree_MatchesChrome`

Asserts that the centers (0, 0) and (100, 0) give the start (15, 0), the middle (50, 0), and the end (85, 0), and the
centers (10, 20) and (40, 60) the start (19, 32), the middle (25, 40), and the end (31, 48) (to 1e-12); and that
centers 10 or exactly 15 apart give NaN points, cytoscape's bad line.

##### CanvasNetMermaid-Cose-CoseLayout-BrowserFidelity: Chrome's Layouts Bit For Bit

**Tests**: `CoseGoldenTests.CoseLayout_Run_GoldenTree_MatchesChrome`

For each of the 36 recorded layouts (a theory with one case each: one node, two nodes, two nodes of size 0, a path of
40, a star of 60, a binary tree of 63, a ternary tree of 40, a chain of depth 32, random trees of 3 to 600 nodes, bushy
trees, a tree with shuffled edges, zero widths, identical sizes, sizes beyond the world boundary, huge sizes, sizes past
the plain number strings, an infinite size, and three trees replayed from mindmaps drawn by Mermaid), lays out the input
without a work limit and asserts the iteration count and every center and edge point coordinate equal to Chrome's (NaN
where Chrome gives NaN), compared with `CompareTo`; and that each of the 3 cases Chrome cannot lay out fails with a
`MermaidRenderException` of code `mermaid-render-failed`.

##### CanvasNetMermaid-Cose-CoseLayout-Math: Correctly Rounded Math Equal To Chrome's

**Tests**: `CoseLayoutTests.CoseLayout_SinCos_SpecialCases_FollowJavaScriptAndDomain`,
`CoseLayoutTests.CoseLayout_Log_SpecialCases_FollowJavaScriptAndDomain`,
`CoseLayoutTests.CoseLayout_Pow_SpecialCases_FollowV8AndDomain`,
`CoseLayoutTests.CoseLayout_RoundToDouble_Values_RoundToNearestEven`,
`CoseLayoutTests.CoseLayout_RoundCorrectly_NearMidpoint_Escalates`,
`CoseLayoutTests.CoseLayout_SinCos_ChromeVectors_BitEqual`,
`CoseLayoutTests.CoseLayout_Log_ChromeVectors_BitEqual`,
`CoseLayoutTests.CoseLayout_Pow_ChromeCoolingPairs_BitEqual`

Asserts that `sin(-0)` is -0, `sin(0)` is 0, `cos(-0)` is 1, the sine is odd and the cosine even, the sine of the
smallest subnormal is itself, and NaN, 8.5, and negative infinity are refused with an `ArgumentOutOfRangeException`;
that `log(1)` is exactly 0 without a further precision step, the logarithms of the smallest subnormal and the largest
double are -744.4400719213812 and 709.782712893384, and 0, -1, infinity, and NaN are refused; that the power follows
V8's and ECMAScript's special cases (a NaN exponent, -1 to an infinite power, `(-3)^2` as 9, `9^0.5` as 3, `-∞^0.5` as
+∞, `(-0)^0.5` as +0, `NaN^0` as 1, `NaN^3` as NaN, `1^1234.5` as 1), gives the exact powers 1024, 8, and 0.1,
overflows to +∞ and underflows to 0 and the smallest subnormal as IEEE 754 does, and refuses a negative or zero base
and an infinite exponent; that rounding to the nearest double breaks ties to even (2^53 + 1 to 2^53 and 2^53 + 3 to
2^53 + 4), gives subnormal results, keeps the sign of a zero, and overflows to infinity; that a value just above a
rounding midpoint (1 + 2^-53 + 2^-300) is decided at the second precision, one step, as 1.0000000000000002, while a value
exactly on a midpoint cannot be rounded; and that the sine and cosine of all 23,480 angles, the logarithm of all 5,002
values, and the power of all 850 cooling pairs equal Chrome's results bit for bit.

##### CanvasNetMermaid-Cose-CoseLayout-Work: Pinned Work And The Limit

**Tests**: `CoseLayoutTests.CoseLayout_Run_SmallTrees_ChargesPinnedWork`,
`CoseLayoutTests.CoseLayout_Run_SmallBudget_LimitExceeded`

Asserts that the layout of the two-node tree charges exactly 903 units and that of a path of ten nodes exactly 5,598;
and that a star of ten nodes under a budget of 1,000 units fails with `mermaid-limit-exceeded` at the meter's location.

##### CanvasNetMermaid-Cose-CoseLayout-Failures: Grids JavaScript Cannot Build

**Tests**: `CoseLayoutTests.CoseLayout_Run_GridFailures_ThrowsRenderFailed`

Asserts that a node 1e24 wide beside a node of 50 by 40, which lies outside the repulsion grid (a `TypeError` in
Mermaid), fails with `mermaid-render-failed` ("outside the repulsion grid") at the meter's location, and that an
infinitely wide node, whose grid JavaScript cannot build (a `RangeError`), fails with `mermaid-render-failed` ("cannot
be built").

##### CanvasNetMermaid-Cose-CoseLayout-Cancellation: A Canceled Call Stops

**Tests**: `CoseLayoutTests.CoseLayout_Run_Canceled_ThrowsOperationCanceled`

Asserts that a layout of three nodes with a canceled token and no work limit stops with an
`OperationCanceledException`.
